# Got Five! Rules

Got Five! is a game of logic and deduction where players compete to be the first to correctly guess their five hidden numbers.

## Goal
Be the first to correctly guess your 5 hidden numbers using the clues revealed over the course of the game!

## Components
- 60 Tiles: 5 colors, numbered 1 to 60. Each tile has 1, 2, or 3 dots.
- 4 Stands: Each with 5 tile slots and a sorting zone with 6 notches.
- 4 Screens: To hide your game board.
- 4 Game Boards and Dry-Erase Markers: To track your deductions.

## Setup
1. Prepare Supplies: Shuffle all 60 tiles face down in the center. These form the supply.
2. Personal Setup: Each player takes a screen, game board, marker, and stand. Place the board and marker behind your screen.
3. Draw Hidden Tiles: Each player draws 1 tile of each of the 5 colors at random from the supply.
4. Arrange Stand: Ask an opponent to place your 5 tiles into the slots on your stand. They must arrange them in ascending order - smallest on the left, largest on the right - facing away from you.
   - Important: You must never look at the numbers on your own tiles.
5. Public Tiles: Draw 1 tile of each color at random and place them face up near the supply. These are the initial clues.
6. Initial Deduction: On your game board, cross out all numbers visible on your opponents stands and the 5 face-up tiles near the supply.

## How to Play
The youngest player starts. Play continues clockwise. Each turn consists of two actions in order:

### 1. Reveal a Tile
Draw 1 face-down tile of any color from the supply. Flip it face up, announce its number, and place it with the other face-up tiles near the supply.

### 2. Ask for a Clue
Select any 1 tile from those face up near the supply. Use it to ask your neighbor for one of the following two clues:

- SORT: Ask your neighbor to sort the tile relative to your 5 hidden tiles. Your neighbor must place the tile in one of the 6 notches on your stand to show where it would fit in the ascending sequence of your hidden numbers.
- COMPARE: Hold the tile above one of the 5 tiles on your stand. Ask your neighbor if the two tiles have the same number of dots. They must answer Yes or No.
  - Tip: Place the tile in front of the corresponding slot - straight for Yes, angled for No - to remember the clue.

After each action, all players should update their game boards with any deductions they can make.

## End of the Game
You have a single chance to guess your 5 numbers. You can announce your guess at any time, even if it is not your turn.

1. Shout GOT FIVE!.
2. List your guesses for your 5 tiles in ascending order.
3. Opponents confirm your guesses:
   - Correct: You win the game immediately!
   - Incorrect: You lose the game and are out. The remaining players continue until only one is left or someone guesses correctly.
